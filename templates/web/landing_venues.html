What you get

Written from behind the booking desk

Not another newsletter about how great live music is. This one is about making the room work.

The Numbers

Guarantees versus splits, settlement terms, bar margin, and staffing costs — with the arithmetic shown, because a sold-out show can still lose money.

The Room

Sound, sightlines, load-in logistics, backline, and turnarounds. Hands-on production writing from engineers who have rebuilt a PA on a budget that shouldn't have worked.

The Paperwork

Licensing, noise ordinances, insurance, planning objections, and grant deadlines — jurisdiction-aware, never pretending a rule is universal when it isn't.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is this for?

Venue owners and general managers, talent buyers, independent promoters, and the production and front-of-house staff who make the night happen. If you have ever had to settle a show at 1am, it's for you.

What's in each issue?

The Door (deal economics), Booking Desk (calendars, advances, routing), and Packed House (local marketing and day-of-show) run in every issue, plus rotating coverage of production, hospitality, licensing, community relations, and independent-venue advocacy.

How often will I hear from you?

Three times a week by default — and you can drop to twice or once a week from your preferences page at any time.

Is it really free?

Yes, completely free. No paywalls, no trials, no credit card required.
